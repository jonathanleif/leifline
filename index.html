01 — WHAT WE HANDLE

Most matters reach Leifline after every reasonable explanation has already been attempted.

S/01

Unexplained developments

Something has happened. Nobody involved is able to say why, and the matter is not improving on its own.

S/02

Disputed outcomes

You believe you are right. The other party is unusually confident of the opposite. A finding is required.

S/03

Decisions of the small hours

Matters that feel manageable at 14:00 and unmanageable at 02:14. Leifline maintains full capacity at both times.

S/04

Preventive assessment

Before answering, sending, buying, resigning or celebrating, a preliminary assessment may be requested.

SCOPE

Domestic, professional, romantic, vehicular, administrative and unclassifiable matters. No matter has yet been declined on the grounds of being too small.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

Therapy, legal representation, mediation or extended reasoning. Leifline issues findings. The finding is the service.

RESPONSE MEDIUM

Proprietary. Leifline does not disclose the instrument used to answer and has never been required to. Availability during the last reporting period: 100.0%.

02 — THE METHOD

Every matter is handled in exactly the same way, regardless of how unusual the caller considers it to be.

The procedure below has not been revised since 2019. Proposals to shorten it have been considered and rejected.

I

The call is answered

Before the second ring. No greeting is used; a greeting delays the matter and has so far assisted no one.

II

The account is heard

Up to ninety seconds, uninterrupted. Nothing is written down. Everything is remembered, including what the caller omits.

III

The assessment is made

Usually during the account rather than after it. Deliberation continues only where its absence would be experienced as unsettling.

IV

The finding is delivered

In one sentence. Repetition is available on request. The wording does not change on repetition.

The matter is considered resolved when the finding has been delivered, not when the caller agrees with it.

07 — ABOUT LEIFLINE

An independent authority for situations that do not resolve themselves.

Leifline was founded by Jonathan in response to a growing need for clear findings in situations characterised by uncertainty, disagreement and confusion that could have been avoided. What began as an informal point of contact has developed into an independent service operating at all hours of the day, in every district, and within every category of matter so far encountered.

All matters receive the same personal handling, as there are currently no other employees.

Jonathan
FOUNDER · HEAD OF INTERPRETATION · FINAL AUTHORITY

CLARITY

One sentence is sufficient for most problems.

CONSISTENCY

Every finding is consistently regarded as correct.

AVAILABILITY

Leifline is available when Jonathan answers.

DISCRETION

What is said on the line stays on the line, including the parts that were shouted.

You do not need to understand the situation. You only need to know who to call.

Leifline is primarily a telephone service. Written matters are received, read and — where deemed appropriate — answered.
