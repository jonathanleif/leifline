05 / The process

From incident
to resolution.

  1. 01

    You experience a problem

    A point is awarded, a result is questioned or a rule becomes difficult to interpret.

  2. 02

    Jonathan assesses the situation

    Available evidence is considered alongside the broader needs of the system.

  3. 03

    A decision is communicated

    You receive a clear outcome and, where appropriate, a revised point position.

  4. 04

    The matter is considered resolved

    Further correspondence may result in a new and entirely separate assessment.
